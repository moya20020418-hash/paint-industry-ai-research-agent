# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or industry.

This project is particularly aimed at supporting non-engineer business users in traditional industries. Contributions that improve accessibility, usability, and documentation for non-technical users are especially valued.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other community members, including non-engineer users

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or insulting/derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Dismissing contributions because they come from non-engineers or non-technical backgrounds
- Other conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, and Discussions.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a GitHub Issue. Project maintainers will review and respond to all reports.
